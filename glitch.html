Glitch Art

2026

Medium / Tools: Text Editor Databending, Audacity

This project explores post-hardcore and alternative rock music through digital glitching. I used album artwork from Deftones and Pierce the Veil and experimented with text-editor databending and Audacity audio processing. The glitches translate the raw, aggressive sound of the music into visual distortion and digital noise. Chance also became part of the process because changing the files could create unpredictable results.
