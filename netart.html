SAVE FILE is a web-based artwork inspired by video game save files, memories, and corrupted digital files. The viewer explores memories connected to games such as Minecraft, Mortal Kombat, Ghost of Tsushima, and Ghost of Yotei. The hyperlinks allow the viewer to remember, forget, restore, and revisit memories through branching and looping navigation.
